# Kyro X 1.0.133 — podcast device acceptance

Build 133 adds local podcast discovery, subscriptions, playback and downloads to the existing Radio route. It does not require a Silo server change. The current Android, Roku and Web clients are not changed; the Apple cassette interface and its iOS media controls are platform-specific. Cross-client podcast parity is deferred until there is a pull request to link.

## Host and network verification

- Run `bash scripts/run-build133-host-checks.sh` for RSS and OPML fixtures, discovery decoding, cache and offline file lookup, portable backup, queue and sleep policy, exclusivity routing, cassette motion policy, and Home state isolation.
- Add `--live` for a bounded US creator search and public RSS fetch through the production client. Apple's [Search API documentation](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/AudioVideo/Conceptual/iTuneSearchAPI/Searching.html) specifies a country/storefront and describes the approximate 20-requests-per-minute service limit. Kyro uses explicit search only, caps results, spaces requests, caches results briefly, and links back to Apple Podcasts. Results without a public feed are not opened.
- An unsigned generic iOS Release build is compile evidence only. The checks above do not exercise the AVPlayer audio route, the display, the physical keyboard and navigation layout, or iPhone frame pacing.

## On an iPhone 16 Plus

1. **Entry points:** Open Radio & Podcasts through More and then as the fourth tab. Switch between Radio and Podcasts. Check Radio Discover, Library, and History before and after switching. Confirm the floating pills still look and behave the same. Test with the keyboard open and in a pushed copy of the page. Verify Podcasts also opens from server setup and sign-in without creating or using an account.
2. **Discovery and offline library:** Search once by a show name and once by its creator. Open the result and verify show artwork, publisher, and descriptions. Subscribe, search and sort the library, refresh, and unsubscribe. Add a public RSS URL directly; verify a malformed feed shows an error. Export subscriptions to Files, import the file twice, and confirm there are no duplicates. Reopen a cached subscription in Airplane Mode.
3. **Playback:** Play a public episode with artwork. Check mini-player continuity across tabs and inside the pushed audio destination. Pause, resume, seek, use both skip controls, test 0.5× and 3×, mark played/unplayed, save, and verify position after relaunch. Reorder and remove queue items, and confirm the next episode starts only when permitted by the sleep timer. Test chapters on a feed that publishes Podcasting 2.0 JSON chapters.
4. **Audio routes:** Verify Lock Screen and Control Center controls and background playback, then test Bluetooth disconnect and AirPlay. Start Radio, an audiobook, Silo video, Live TV, and a Sports stream individually while a podcast is playing; repeat in the other direction. Expect only one local source to play.
5. **Downloads:** Observe progress, cancel and retry, and confirm Wi-Fi-only transfers do not proceed over cellular. Download an episode, relaunch in Airplane Mode, and play and scrub it. Remove an episode and confirm storage changes. Enable removal after completion and test it on a short episode.
6. **Cassette:** Check Crimson Noir and Classic Walnut separately, including artwork and long show/episode titles. Reels should spin only during real playback or a seek, and should stop when paused, dismissed, or backgrounded or with Reduce Motion enabled. Scrub near the start, middle, and end to check the tape moves between reels. Verify the two Radio turntables are unchanged and the two theme preferences are independent.
7. **Home and backup:** Compare Home against accepted Build 131 with podcast shelves off, then enable each shelf separately. Scroll with a podcast playing and confirm no new hitches or sports-card clipping. Restore an older settings backup; it should not reset Podcast preferences. Back up a public-directory subscription with a saved episode and resume position. Restore it and re-fetch the feed; confirm no downloads, private feed addresses, queue, or listening history transferred.

## Limits

Public RSS enclosures play in formats supported by iOS. Private or authenticated feeds and Apple-exclusive audio are not supported. The directory can vary by storefront or become unavailable; direct RSS is independent of it. A feed snapshot carries up to 300 playable episodes. Automatic refresh runs in bounded batches when Podcasts is open in the foreground, not as a background crawler. Started downloads are user-initiated foreground transfers, not guaranteed to survive app suspension or termination. Chapters currently use the Podcasting 2.0 JSON extension, not every embedded format. Portable Settings Backup includes eligible public-directory subscriptions only, not unverified direct or OPML URLs. OPML is an explicit export of subscription addresses; share it only with people who should see those links.
